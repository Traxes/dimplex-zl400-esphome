A drop-in replacement for the wall control panel (“Bedienelement Typ 1”) of **Dimplex ZL 300 VF / ZL 400 VF** home ventilation units. A Seeed **XIAO ESP32-C6** reads the unit's five 24 V status outputs through optocouplers and “presses” the up/down buttons with optocoupler-driven PNP switches. In Home Assistant the unit shows up as a normal fan with three speeds.

**Highlights**

- Galvanically isolated: the ESP is powered only by USB-C, nothing but optocouplers crosses to the unit's 24 V side.
- Closed-loop control: every key press is verified through the unit's LED outputs; retries with back-off, fault entities for HA.
- KiCad 10 project + ready-to-order JLCPCB files (Gerber, BOM, CPL).
- Snap-fit 3D-printed enclosure without screws, generated by a parametric Fusion 360 script.
- Worst-case analysis, SPICE Monte Carlo and FMEA in [`docs/qa`](docs/qa) (German).

**Status (2026-10-19):** installed and running in a ZL 400 VF. Live test off → 1 → 3 → 2 → off: LED feedback within 3 s for every step, no fault flags.

> [!WARNING]
> The ventilation unit contains mains voltage (230 V). Read the **[safety notes](#safety)** before you open the unit or connect anything. Build at your own risk. This is not a Dimplex product.

## Safety

> [!CAUTION]
> Working on the ventilation unit can be **life-threatening**: there is mains voltage (230 V) inside. Read this section completely before you start.

**Electrical**

- Before opening the unit or the terminal compartment, disconnect it from mains **on all poles** (circuit breaker or isolating switch). Secure it against being switched back on and verify that it is dead. Work on mains wiring is for qualified electricians only.
- The controller is designed **only for the unit's 24 V DC control circuit** (PCB3 terminals `+`, `−`, `AUS_x`, `EIN_x`).
  - Never connect any terminal to mains or to another voltage source.
  - The absolute maximum is 30 V; above 27.5 V parts run outside their derating limits.
- Measure `+` to `−` on PCB3 (DC volts) and check every wire against the [wiring diagram](#wiring) before you connect the controller. Swapping `+` and `−` is blocked by diodes; other wiring mistakes are not.
- Use **ferrules** on stranded wires, strip only as much as the terminal needs, and pull-test every wire. A stray strand can short the unit's 24 V supply.
- Leave all other PCB3 terminals untouched. In particular, keep the **RM (smoke detector) bridge** in place: it is a safety shutdown input of the unit.
- Do not connect other loads to the unit's 24 V supply. Its transformer is only rated for a few control panels and sensors (Dimplex manual, table 4.1).

**ESP / USB side**

- Power the XIAO only from a **certified USB power supply** (safety extra-low voltage, CE marked). Never feed it from the unit's 24 V.
- Never connect USB ground or any ESP pin to the unit's wiring. The isolation between the two sides must stay intact.

**Enclosure and location**

- The printed enclosure is **not flame-retardant**: PLA and PETG are not UL 94 V-0. The controller dissipates only milliwatts, but keep it away from heat sources and out of the air stream of electric heater coils.
- PLA softens from about 55 °C; use PETG or ASA for warm locations.
- Install only in dry, frost-free rooms (the same requirements as for the unit itself): no condensation, no outdoor use.
- Close the lid before operation; it protects the terminals against accidental contact.

**Ventilation function**

- Home ventilation protects the building against moisture and mould. Make sure your automations **can never keep the unit off for long periods**, and keep the minimum ventilation required for your building (in Germany, for example, the moisture-protection level of DIN 1946-6).
- If a **fireplace or other combustion appliance** shares the air volume, the safety devices required for that combination (for example a pressure monitor) stay mandatory. This controller does not replace them, and remote control must never override them.
- The unit's own protective functions (frost protection, smoke-detector shutdown, filter alarm) stay active. Do not bypass them.
- A Wi-Fi or firmware fault can leave the unit at its last stage. Watch `control_failed` and `feedback_lost` in Home Assistant; the unit's own display and buttons keep working as a fallback.

**Liability**

- Building and using this is at your own risk. Connecting third-party electronics may affect the warranty of the ventilation unit. The documents come without any guarantee of correctness. This project is not affiliated with Dimplex.

## How it works

![Block diagram](docs/bilder/architecture.svg)

| Direction | Circuit |
|---|---|
| Unit → ESP (AUS0–AUS4) | +24 V → 10 kΩ → EL817 LED → AUS. The unit switches AUS to 0 V, exactly like the LEDs of the original panel (≈ 2 mA). Output side: 3.3 V → GPIO with 10 kΩ pull-down. |
| ESP → unit (EIN0/EIN1) | GPIO → 330 Ω → EL817 → pulls the base of an MMBT3906 (22 kΩ); the PNP puts +24 V on EIN through 1 kΩ – like pressing the panel button. 300 ms pulses. |
| Power | ESP from USB-C only (an ESP powered from the unit's 24 V had tripped error E05 at power-up). 24 V side: 1N4148W reverse-polarity protection in + and −, “24V OK” LED. Load on the unit ≈ 1–10 mA. |

## Wiring

![Wiring diagram](docs/bilder/wiring.svg)

Set the unit to **service menu 20 → 21 Bedienelement → “Typ1”**. Terminal assignment follows the Dimplex installation manual ZL 300/400 VF, chapter 4.5.4, fig. 4.13 ([PDF](https://www.dimplex.eu/sites/g/files/emiian586/files/media_import/medias/docus/5/Dimplex_ZL300VF_Montageanweisung-WP_DE_FD9910.pdf)).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Unlike the original panel, this controller needs the **GND → “−”** wire. Measure between PCB3 “+” and “−” first: the design is rated for 21.6–27 V (see [Known limitations](#known-limitations)).

## Build it

### 1. PCB

1. Upload `hardware/controller-v3/fertigung-jlcpcb/gerbers.zip` to JLCPCB (2 layers, 1.6 mm, 75 × 64 mm), choose PCBA Economic and add the BOM and CPL files. Q1/Q2 (SOT-23) rotation is already corrected by 180° in the CPL. Still check the placement preview.
2. Hand-solder a Seeed **XIAO ESP32-C6** and 5.08 mm screw terminals (2-pin J1, 5-pin J2, 2-pin J3). The XIAO can be soldered directly (the enclosure is dimensioned for that) or plugged into 1×7 socket headers.
3. Commission it step by step with [`docs/Inbetriebnahme_Controller_v3.html`](docs/Inbetriebnahme_Controller_v3.html) (download and open locally; it covers measuring points, multimeter settings and expected values; German).

| 1N4148W SOD-123 | C81598 | 7 | D1–D5, D7, D8 |
| LED green 0805 | C2297 | 1 | D6 |
| MMBT3906 SOT-23 | C2143 | 2 | Q1, Q2 |
| EL817S SMD-4 | C63268 | 7 | U1–U7 |
| 10 kΩ 0805 | C17414 | 14 | R1–R10, R15, R16, R18, R19 |

### 2. Firmware

1. Copy `firmware/secrets.example.yaml` to `secrets.yaml` and fill it in. Never commit it, it is in `.gitignore`.
2. Add `trax_ha_c6.yaml` in the ESPHome dashboard and flash it over USB. If you get “Failed to connect”, hold the **B (BOOT)** button next to the USB socket while plugging in the cable. After that, OTA works.
3. Adopt the device in Home Assistant (ESPHome integration).

| Entity | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `fan.<device>_ventilation` | 3 speeds: 33 / 66 / 100 %, off = unit off |
| `sensor.<device>_ventilation_level` | Actual stage 0–3 from the unit's LEDs |
| `sensor.<device>_ventilation_status` | OK / adjusting / fault text |
| `binary_sensor.<device>_ventilation_auto`, `…_error` | Auto mode, fault/filter LED |
| `…_control_failed`, `…_feedback_lost`, `…_stuck_key_suspected` | Diagnostics |
| `sensor.<device>_ventilation_uncommanded_changes` | Stage changes not caused by HA |
| `button.<device>_ventilation_clear_faults` | Reset diagnostics |

#### In Home Assistant

<p align="center"><img src="docs/bilder/home_assistant_geraet.png" width="420" alt="Home Assistant device page with fan dialog, sensors and activity log"></p>
<p align="center"><sub>Device page with the fan dialog. The activity log shows a real command from an automation: fan on at 22:10:00, LED feedback for stage 1 in the same second, status “OK” at 22:10:02.</sub></p>

- The fan dialog offers **Off / Low / Medium / High**: stages 0–3, 0 / 33 / 66 / 100 %.
- The status sensor uses German texts:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OK` | Idle, stage confirmed by the unit's LEDs |
| `Stellt ein: 1 → 2` | Adjusting from stage 1 to 2 |
| `Aus (oder 24 V fehlt)` | Unit is off – or there is no 24 V / no LED signal at all |
| `Gestört/unbekannt – keine Rückmeldung` | A key press caused no LED change (check 24 V, wiring, EIN) |
| `Steuerung fehlgeschlagen` | Target not reached after all retries |
| `Verdacht Dauerdruck – Hardware prüfen (T2)` | Stage moved against the pressed direction – possible stuck key |
| `Start` | Booting; key presses are blocked until the first sync |

Tested with ESPHome 2026.9.1 on a XIAO ESP32-C6.

**Control logic:** A requested speed becomes single key presses (300 ms pulse, 700 ms pause). After each press the firmware waits up to 2 s for the stage LEDs to change. If they don't, it retries with back-off (1/2/4/4 s, max. 10 presses). On boot the entity syncs to the real stage without pressing anything.

```yaml
# Example automation action
action: fan.set_percentage
target:
  entity_id: fan.trax_ha_c6_ventilation
data:
  percentage: 66   # 33 = stage 1, 66 = stage 2, 100 = stage 3; use fan.turn_off for off
```

LED feedback is immediate. The fans themselves need about 30–70 s to reach their speed.

- **Size:** 80.7 × 69.8 × 24.1 mm, 95.8 mm long including the two wall-mounting tabs (slots for Ø 4 screws).
- **No hardware needed:** pins on the bosses locate the PCB, four hold-downs in the lid clamp it, and the lid snaps into four windows. To open it, push each hook inwards through its window with a small screwdriver.
- **Openings:** nine labelled wire holes (Ø 3.9 mm, aligned with the terminal entries), a USB-C opening, pin holes for RESET/BOOT (paper clip) and a light pipe for “24V OK”.
- **Printing** (tested on a Bambu Lab P2S, PLA): 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 4 bottom layers, 15 % infill, no supports, lid printed top-down. About 1 h 25 min and 49 g. Use PETG for warm locations.
- **Files:**
  - `CentralVentilationCase_BambuStudio_P2S_PLA.3mf`: ready-to-print Bambu Studio project
  - `…_Geometrie.3mf`: for other slicers
  - single STL files
  - STEP assembly with a PCB dummy
- **Modify:** [`gehaeuse/fusion/gehaeuse_fusion.py`](gehaeuse/fusion/gehaeuse_fusion.py) rebuilds the whole model in Fusion 360 via the API. All dimensions are at the top of the script; the measurements come from the [dimension sheet](docs/bilder/massblatt_controller_v3.png).

## Known limitations

Details are in [`docs/qa/QA_Verifikation_2026-10-15.md`](docs/qa/QA_Verifikation_2026-10-15.md) (German).

- **Supply voltage:** designed to EEE-INST-002 derating for 21.6–30 V. Above **27.5 V** the output optocouplers U6/U7 exceed the derating limit (absolute max 35 V), and above ≈ 29 V so do R1–R5. Spikes on the 24 V line have not been measured.
- **EIN input current** of the unit has not been measured. It decides whether R20/R21 = 1 kΩ is optimal (up to 4 mA the unit still sees ≥ 19 V).
- **Short circuit on EIN** is tolerable for the pulse length only: a permanent short would put 0.5 W into R20/R21, which are rated 0.125 W.
- **A stuck key is not single-fault safe:** a shorted Q1/Q2 or U6/U7 would hold a button down. The firmware only detects it indirectly (`stuck_key_suspected`).
